## 3. Critical finding F-045 — BUY fills raced graceful shutdown

<badge color="danger">HIGH</badge> **Confirmed shutdown lifecycle gap — tracked in issue #1097.**

Shutdown began at **03:51:04.844 UTC**. Two already-acknowledged BUY orders then filled while shutdown was in progress:

| Market | BUY order / trade | Fill | Existing recovery SELL left open |
|---|---|---|---|
| WLDFDUSD | 341640869 / 5779724 | 20 WLD @ 0.2885; 5.77 FDUSD; 03:51:24.400Z | 341640866: 219.9 WLD @ 0.2978 |
| ADAFDUSD | 780132097 / 16096399 | 30 ADA @ 0.168; 5.04 FDUSD; 03:51:25.088Z | 780132095: 359.9 ADA @ 0.172 |

The fills were applied. No shutdown cancellation request was recorded for either BUY, and no cancellation/replacement request was recorded for either existing SELL.

### Why this happened

The exact runtime source has this shutdown sequence:

1. \`create-shutdown-handler.js:52-54\` sets the stopped flag.
2. \`create-shutdown-handler.js:107-143\` drains market work, reconciles owned orders, then cancels BUY orders that are still active.
3. \`conservative-trading-engine.js:1714-1723\` selects only registry BUY orders in OPEN or PARTIALLY_FILLED state for shutdown cancellation.
4. \`order-event-workflow.js:306-308\` returns early when \`isStopping()\` is true, before the normal BUY-fill path recalculates the recovery SELL.
5. \`create-shutdown-handler.js:167-176\` allows open SELL orders and does not verify their quantity or proceeds against the final Position/RecoveryLedger before closing.

The shutdown handler reconciles before its BUY-cancellation pass. If a BUY fills in that window, it becomes terminal and is no longer eligible for cancellation. The fill handler deliberately skips recovery-SELL replacement because the process is stopping. The exact start/end time of the reconciliation and cancellation stages is not logged, so the attachments cannot pinpoint the fill relative to the internal call boundary. They do prove both fills happened after the stop flag was set, no cancellation requests followed, and the old SELLs remained last-observed OPEN.

The CLOSING snapshot at 03:55:30.169Z nevertheless reported \`auditStatus: COMPLETE\` and \`auditReasons: []\`. The shutdown audit permits open SELLs but does not check that they still cover the final active position and recovery amount. This is a false-safe completion signal for this session.
